Our History · Past Masters

Past Masters, 1825 to present

Washington Lodge has a long tradition of each brother being elected Master only once. No brother has served more than one term since 1900, longer than any other lodge in the state. That is no comment on the abilities of those Masters; it is a testament to how many men have been willing and able to take on the responsibility.
