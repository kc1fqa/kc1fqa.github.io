About Freemasonry

A personal journey, shared with brothers.

Freemasonry is the world's oldest and largest fraternity, formally organized in London in 1717 and counting millions of members today. At its heart it is a path toward becoming a better husband, father, friend and citizen, walked alongside men who are on that same road.

The lodge room is where that work happens: a place for Masonic education, fellowship, and men of different backgrounds to meet on common ground.

Fellowship

Lodge dinners, cookouts and outings that turn members into friends.

Charity

Blood drives, scholarships and support for neighbors in Windsor.

Education

Degree work and Masonic study for every stage of the journey.

Tradition

Customs like our St. John's Day observance, kept since the 1800s.

Our History

1825 to today

Washington Lodge received its charter in 1825 and marked its bicentennial in 2025. Our brothers come not only from Windsor but from across Connecticut and beyond. Our Historian has compiled a full account of the lodge's first two centuries, along with a roll of every Worshipful Master since 1825.

We are also proud members of the Society of Washington Lodges, supporting the George Washington Masonic National Memorial.

A lodge tradition: since 1900, no brother has served more than one term as Worshipful Master, a longer run than any other lodge in Connecticut. It speaks to how many men have stepped up to lead.
